**Author:** Juan Moisés de la Serna Tuya

## What is this dataset?

This dataset compiles results from behavioral economics and neuroeconomics experiments published between 1990 and 2025. It integrates findings across four core experimental paradigms: the Ultimatum Game, the Iowa Gambling Task (IGT), Risk Aversion Ta

## Why does it matter?

This dataset contributes to open science by making research data freely available for reuse, analysis, and citation.

## Who can use it?

- Researchers and academics
- Policy makers
- Journalists and science communicators
- Students and educators
- General public

## How to use it?

1. Download the data from [GitHub](https://github.com/juanmoisesd/global-behavioral-economics-experiments-dataset)
2. Open with Excel, R, Python or any data analysis tool
3. Cite using the CITATION.cff or CITATION.bib files
