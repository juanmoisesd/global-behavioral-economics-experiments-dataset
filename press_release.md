### New Open Dataset Published: This dataset compiles results from behavioral economics and neuroeconomics experiments published between 1990 and 2025. It integrates findings across four core experimental paradigms: the Ultimatum Game, the Iowa Gambling Task (IGT), Risk Aversion Ta

**Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR)** — Juan Moisés de la Serna Tuya (ORCID: https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8401-8018) has published a new open dataset: *This dataset compiles results from behavioral economics and neuroeconomics experiments published between 1990 and 2025. It integrates findings across four core experimental paradigms: the Ultimatum Game, the Iowa Gambling Task (IGT), Risk Aversion Ta*.

The dataset is freely available under the **CC0 1.0 Universal** license, allowing unrestricted use, distribution, and reproduction.
